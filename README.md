# ⚓ Nocturnal Navigator
### Elevate Your Maritime Experience

**Nocturnal Navigator** is a premium, high-fidelity yacht charter platform designed for those who demand the extraordinary. Built with a "Midnight Horizon" dark luxury aesthetic, the platform offers a cinematic browsing experience with smooth inertial scrolling, scroll-triggered reveals, and interactive micro-animations.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Cinematic Design**: A bespoke dark mode interface with vibrant primary accents (`#ff5f1f`) and smooth gradients.
- **Premium Fluid Interactivity**: 
  - **Lenis Smooth Scroll**: Elegant, high-performance scrolling transitions.
  - **GSAP Animations**: Scroll-triggered text reveals and parallax effects.
  - **Interactive Cursor Glow**: A subtle, reactive glow that follows the user's focus.
  - **Shiny CTA Effects**: Modern, attention-grabbing interactive elements.
- **Curated Fleet**: Detailed showcasing of Superyachts, Sport Cruisers, Classic Sail, and Interior Suites.
- **Global Destinations**: Explore elite locations like Monaco, Saint-Tropez, and the Amalfi Coast.
- **Bespoke Concierge**: Integrated inquiry systems for private events and culinary experiences.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3
- **Framework & Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Configuration optimized for dark luxury)
- **Animation Libraries**: 
  - [Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) for smooth scrolling.
  - [GSAP](https://greensock.com/gsap/) for advanced motion design.
- **Icons & Typography**: Google Fonts (Noto Serif, Manrope) and Material Symbols.
